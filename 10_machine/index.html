<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h1>👷🏼‍♀️ Weeks 10-12: Machine Building 👷</h1> <br></br> <h2 class="p1">Part I: Build a Drawing Robot!</h2> <p><em>Submission for Amanda Tu, Xander Patton, Suhani Sharma, and Yuhan Ye.</em></p> <br></br> <p class="p1">This week&rsquo;s assignment was to assemble a drawing machine capable of rendering any user-defined shape and homing reliably to a consistent origin. We started with a basic hardware kit and a starter codebase, which we were free to adapt as needed.</p> <p class="p1">During our initial group brainstorm, we explored a range of ideas&mdash;including Spirograph-style patterns&mdash;but ultimately drew inspiration from a water-marbling project Yuhan had created during her GSD program. That led us to reimagine the drawing machine less as a plotting device and more as an art piece: an <span class="s2"><strong>Ephemeral Drawing Machine</strong></span> that dispenses ink into water to create transient, marbled patterns that dissipate over time.</p> <p class="p1">We divided responsibilities as follows:</p> <ul> <li class="p1"><strong>Hardware assembly:</strong><span class="s1"> Entire group</span></li> <li> <p class="p1"><strong>Ink pen end-effector design and fabrication:</strong><span class="s1"> Amanda</span></p> </li> <li> <p class="p1"><strong>Structural stand for the drawing rig:</strong><span class="s1"> Xander</span></p> </li> <li> <p class="p1"><strong>Coding and wiring:</strong><span class="s1"> Suhani and Yuhan</span></p> </li> </ul> <p class="p1">Designing and fabricating the ink-dispensing end effector was relatively straightforward. We purchased a watercolor pen with a built-in reservoir that releases ink when pressed, then designed and 3D-printed a custom collar that both anchors the fastening screw and applies the necessary pressure for ink flow.</p> <p class="p1">For the platform, we constructed a simple wooden stand to mount the drawing rig, elevating it above the water reservoir where the marbling ink would be deposited.</p> <p class="p1">Most of our challenges centered on the basic hardware kit and the coding / wiring. Key issues included:</p> <ul> <li> <p class="p1"><span class="s1"><strong>Belt slippage:</strong></span> The belt repeatedly slipped until we realized it was oriented incorrectly. After flipping it to ensure proper tooth meshing, we had to apply extremely firm tension (three people pulling) and secure it with two zip ties.</p> </li> <li> <p class="p1"><strong>Wiring challenges:</strong> TBD TBD TBD</p> </li> </ul> </div>
